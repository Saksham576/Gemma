# Upload kit: the three pilot Shorts

Files: `out/toast/toast.mp4`, `out/tower/tower.mp4`, `out/cloud/cloud.mp4`. Each is 1080×1920, 24 fps, AAC stereo at −14 LUFS, and loops seamlessly. Suggested cover frames are in `out/<short>/cover.jpg`. In the YouTube app, pick the matching moment as the Short's thumbnail.

For every upload:
- **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids".
- **Altered or synthetic content:** "No". It's procedural animation, not realistic AI footage of real people or events.
- **Music:** none from the library. The soundtrack is original and part of the file.

## Posting order and cadence

Post one every 2–3 days, at the same time of day: **Toast, then Tower, then Cloud**. Leave each Short alone for 48–72 h before judging it.

## Titles, descriptions, hashtags

Keep titles short and curiosity-led. The video tells the joke, so don't spoil the twist in the title.

**Toast**
- Title: `waiting for toast like… 🍞`
- Description: `he waited. he really waited. #shorts #animation #cartoon #toast #funny`

**Tower**
- Title: `one more block… 😬`
- Description: `perfectly balanced. until. #shorts #animation #cartoon #satisfying #funny`

**Cloud**
- Title: `his own personal rain cloud ☁️`
- Description: `nature always finds a way. #shorts #animation #cartoon #funny #wholesome`

Pin a comment under each one with a question that invites replies, for example "what should Sak try next? 👇". Comments count as engaged views.

## What to measure (YouTube Studio → the Short → Analytics → Engagement)

| Metric | Healthy for a <30 s Short | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewed vs swiped away** | ≥ 75% viewed (≤ 25% swiped) | Whether the hook works. If more than 70% swipe away, the first 2 s is broken. |
| **Average % viewed** | ≥ 70%, ideally > 100% (loops) | Whether the middle holds attention and the loop pays off |
| **Engaged views / likes / shares** | — | Which gag people pass on |

Each pilot tests one hook type: **suspense** (Toast), **tension** (Tower) and **absurd premise** (Cloud). Make the next five Shorts in the winning hook type, with Sak as the recurring lead. Once a Short passes ~100k views, or one format keeps winning, make a 2–3 minute horizontal episode from that premise (see RESEARCH.md §1).
